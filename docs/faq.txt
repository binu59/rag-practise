Acme Cloud offers three plans: Starter, Pro, and Enterprise. The Starter plan costs 10 dollars per month and 
includes 1 project and 5 GB of storage. The Pro plan costs 30 dollars per month and includes 10 projects and 
100 GB of storage. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and includes unlimited projects, SSO login, and a 
dedicated support manager.

Acme Cloud support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Starter users get email support with a 48 
hour response time. Pro users get email and chat support with a 12 hour response time. Enterprise users get 
a phone line and a dedicated manager.

To cancel a subscription, go to Settings, then Billing, then click Cancel Plan. Refunds are available within 
14 days of the first payment. After that, you keep access until the end of the billing period.
